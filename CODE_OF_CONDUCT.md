# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Code Janitor is an open-source project and welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and professional;
- focus on technical issues rather than individuals;
- provide actionable feedback;
- accept differing viewpoints;
- protect confidential and proprietary information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, trolling, deliberate disruption and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that do not follow this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in exclusion from project spaces.

Concerns should be reported privately to the project maintainer through the contact details associated with the repository.
